# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming,
respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus on the issue, implementation, or documentation rather than people.
- Welcome differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Accept constructive feedback and correct mistakes in good faith.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention,
threats, doxxing, and deliberately disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainers through
GitHub. Reports will be reviewed and handled as fairly and confidentially as
possible. Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict
participation when necessary to protect the community.
